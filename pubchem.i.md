<topic>PubChem</topic> is the *world's largest collection of freely accessible chemical information*
[<cite>Q131902432</cite>,<cite>Q20987307</cite>].

## Entities

The main classes are:

* Metabolite: ligand, e.g. <topic>drug</topic> / drug-like compound
* Assay: measures some property of, for example, the protein-ligand binding

## Data model

The data model is described in the 2015 paper [<cite>Q20987307</cite>].
